What we do

Software consultancy

Design, build and scale connected products. Over a decade of shipping IoT platforms and the firmware, networks and cloud services behind them.

  • IoT and connected product architecture
  • LoRaWAN, sensor networks and gateway fleets
  • Embedded firmware and hardware bring-up
  • Cloud platforms, data pipelines and dashboards
  • Fractional CTO and technical due diligence

3D printing services

Parts, prototypes and short production runs, printed in-house. From a sketch or a CAD file to a finished object in days, not weeks.

  • Rapid prototyping and design iteration
  • Small-batch and on-demand production
  • Design for additive manufacturing
  • Custom fixtures, enclosures and replacement parts
  • Personalised and bespoke printed products

About

Lavoc Limited is an Irish company that brings software and physical making under one roof. We work with founders, engineering teams and businesses who need something designed, built, or made.

Founded and led by Conall Laverty, an engineer who has spent his career building hardware, firmware and software products, and who spends his spare time at the printer.
